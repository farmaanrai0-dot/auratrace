HERB GUIDE

Brief, useful herb knowledge customers can read before they shop.

These are short educational notes about commonly respected herbs. Keep them practical, traditional, and easy to scan.

Ashwagandha

Traditionally used to support stress balance, energy, and recovery. Often chosen by both men and women for busy daily routines.

Brahmi

Commonly valued for focus, mental clarity, and calm concentration. Popular for study periods and mentally demanding work.

Giloy

Traditionally used in wellness routines for general resilience and seasonal support, especially when people want an all-round tonic herb.

Arjuna

Respected in traditional herbal systems for heart-focused wellness support and steady daily balance.

Tulsi

Used for calm, respiratory comfort, and everyday balance. A strong choice for people who like soothing herbal routines.

Shatavari

Often chosen in women’s wellness routines for nourishment and balance, while still being valued more broadly for general vitality.
