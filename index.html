HOW AURATRACE WILL WORK

Two routes, one honest production calendar.

Ready stock

Weekly or next-week orders are fulfilled only from herbs already grown, harvested and available. No unrealistic promises about planting and delivering in days.

Reserved harvests

Shops, practitioners and serious buyers can reserve a future batch. We confirm the crop window, quantity, price and quality requirements before planting.

Batch passport

Each production batch will receive a unique record covering seed source, crop dates, harvest, drying, storage and independent testing where commissioned.

LIVING HERB LIBRARY

Learn from the plant, the record and the evidence.

Farm visits

Future pre-booked visits will show the living plants, growing conditions and post-harvest process. Access and planning approval will come first.

Evidence pages

Each herb page will separate traditional use, human research, authorised claims and known safety concerns. Education is not a promise to treat disease.

Responsible guidance

Questions can be submitted for educational guidance and signposting. Customers should speak to a pharmacist or doctor about conditions, medicines or symptoms.
